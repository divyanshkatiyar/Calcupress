Calcupress combines an elegant **Material You**-inspired interface with powerful **scientific calculations** and an **all-in-one unit converter**, delivering a ⚡️ blazing-fast, lightweight, responsive, and user-friendly experience—all offline on any device 📥.
> No frameworks. No libraries. No dependencies.

---

### ⚙️ 𝐂𝐨𝐫𝐞 𝐅𝐞𝐚𝐭𝐮𝐫𝐞 𝐔𝐩𝐝𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐬

• 𝐃𝐢𝐟𝐟𝐞𝐫𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐢𝐚𝐥 & 𝐈𝐧𝐭𝐞𝐠𝐫𝐚𝐥 𝐂𝐚𝐥𝐜𝐮𝐥𝐮𝐬: Added support for both Indefinite & Definite Integrals ( ∫ ) alongside Differential Calculus (d/dx) operations, and trigonometric functions including inverse & hyperbolic
• 𝐂𝐨𝐦𝐩𝐥𝐞𝐱 𝐂𝐚𝐥𝐜𝐮𝐥𝐮𝐬 (a+ib) : Full complex number support — real & imaginary parts, polar/rectangular conversions (r , ∠θ), and phase angle evaluations im CMPLX mode
• 𝐌𝐚𝐭𝐫𝐢𝐱 & 𝐕𝐞𝐜𝐭𝐨𝐫 𝐀𝐥𝐠𝐞𝐛𝐫𝐚: Dedicated operations for matrix arithmetic, determinants, inverses, transpose, vector dot & cross products, nPr, nCr & n!
• 𝐄𝐪𝐮𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧 𝐒𝐨𝐥𝐯𝐞𝐫: Multi-degree polynomial & system of linear equations
• 𝐕𝐚𝐫𝐢𝐚𝐛𝐥𝐞 & 𝐂𝐨𝐧𝐬𝐭𝐚𝐧𝐭 𝐀𝐬𝐬𝐢𝐠𝐧𝐦𝐞𝐧𝐭 𝐒𝐭𝐨𝐫𝐚𝐠𝐞: Save, manage, and recall custom variables & built-in physical constants across sessions
• 𝐃𝐞𝐝𝐢𝐜𝐚𝐭𝐞𝐝 𝐌𝐨𝐝𝐞 𝐒𝐰𝐢𝐭𝐜𝐡𝐞𝐫 𝐌𝐞𝐧𝐮: Quick-access navigation menu to toggle seamlessly between Scientific, Matrix/Vector, Complex, Calculus, and Unit Conversion
• 𝐃𝐞𝐠 / 𝐑𝐚𝐝 𝐌𝐨𝐝𝐞 𝐓𝐨𝐠𝐠𝐥𝐞: Instant angular unit switching with state indicators for trigonometric calculations
• 𝐒𝐡𝐢𝐟𝐭 𝐭𝐨𝐠𝐠𝐥𝐞 : Introduced shift button for instant switching between trignometric, inverse trignometric & hyperbolic functions like a physical calculator.

• 🧮 **Scientific Calculator**
- ➕ Basic arithmetic operations
- 📐 Trigonometric & inverse trignometric functions (sin, cos, tan
- √ Square root, modulus |x|, 
- Logarithm (log), Natural logarithm (ln), Exponents (xʸ), Factorial (!)
- (π) Pi constant, (e) Euler's constant
- Parentheses support
